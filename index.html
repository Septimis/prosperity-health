Meet your provider

Graham Burdett, PA-C

Nearly 20 years of experience in the medical field, started off with a Master of Medical Science from Yale University and a B.S. in Psychology from Brigham Young University, Graham draws on deep medical training — and real human empathy. He brings years of experience from urgent care, trauma care, orthopedics and general surgery background — and currently serves with the Utah Air National Guard for over a decade. But what really drives him is relationship-based care: Having served an ecclesiastical mission in 4 different countries, he came to learn and appreciate the importance of humanity and found a common thread in all ethnicities for personnel connection. This kept him yearning to find his passion in serving others and discovering their true need. As a father of 5 children, ranging from elementary age to young adults, he understands the dynamics and nuances of family life. He knows how busy and expensive family life can be. He listens to what you need, understanding your life challenges and tailors personal care for you. Whether it’s basic common medical concerns, chronic-disease management, preventive care, skin issues, hormone wellness, mental-health support — he’s in for the long haul.
